🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 1, 2026

1. Overview

Memoji ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered meme generator that analyzes facial expressions to create personalized meme content. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Camera Images: When you click "SNAP" or upload an image, we capture/receive the image for analysis.
  • Generated Content: AI-generated captions and matched memes/GIFs.
  • Local History: Your results are saved in your browser's local storage (encrypted).

3. How We Process Your Data

Microsoft Azure OpenAI (GPT-4 Vision):

  • Your images are sent to Microsoft Azure AI services for facial expression analysis.
  • As per Microsoft's Data Privacy Policy:
  • Your prompts and images are NOT used to train AI models.
  • Your data is NOT shared with OpenAI or other customers.
  • Images are processed in real-time and NOT stored on Microsoft servers (unless abuse is detected).
  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Google Tenor GIF API:

  • We send text search queries (not your images) to Tenor to find matching GIFs.
  • Tenor may collect usage analytics as per Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Local Data Storage

  • Results are stored in your browser's localStorage with AES encryption.
  • Data automatically expires after 7 days.
  • You can delete your history at any time from the Gallery.
  • Storage is limited to ~10 items to prevent browser quota issues.
  • Data never leaves your device unless you share it.

5. What We DO NOT Do

  • ❌ We do NOT create user accounts or collect personal information.
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • ❌ We do NOT store your images on our servers.
  • ❌ We do NOT use cookies for tracking.
  • ❌ We do NOT train AI models with your data.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: View your saved results in the Gallery.
  • Delete: Remove individual items or clear all history.
  • Withdraw Consent: Stop using the app; clear browser data to remove all traces.
  • Export: Download your creations using the Save feature.

7. Security Measures

  • All API communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • API keys are stored securely in server environment variables.
  • Local storage uses AES-256 encryption.
  • No server-side storage of user content.
